# Essays

Readable, narrative pieces that argue a single idea from the project's
material. Written to be handed to someone and discussed, not to be
cited.

## Audience

People who are not reading the book and will not open `knowledge/` —
colleagues, students, anyone the author wants to explain one idea to
in a sitting.

## Scope

One essay per file. Each states a thesis, works it through at least
one concrete example, and says where the argument stops. Essays may be
written in German; the rest of the repository is English, and this
folder is the deliberate exception, because these texts are written
for a specific German-speaking audience.

## Out of scope

Essays are **not an evidence source**. They contain no canonical
decomposition, no variation-point table, no bibliography of their own.
Where an essay leans on a claim from the knowledge base, it names the
file; where an essay produced a finding, the finding is written into
`knowledge/` and the essay cites it back. Nothing in this folder may
be cited by `knowledge/` as support for anything.

An essay is allowed to be wrong in a way the knowledge base is not.
When one turns out to be wrong, the correction goes into `knowledge/`
and the essay gets a note — it is not silently rewritten into
agreement.
