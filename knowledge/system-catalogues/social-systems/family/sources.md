# Family — Sources

Citations for the claims made in this folder. Keys not expanded here
resolve in `knowledge/references/bibliography.md`.

**Convergence note.** Clinical family therapy independently applied
systems theory to the family decades before this project: the
subsystems, boundaries, and hierarchies in the FL-level below are
standard constructs of structural family therapy, and the field's
existence is evidence that control-structure analysis of families is
clinically usable — with a trained therapist, participant consent,
and therapeutic goals, which are exactly the conditions this
catalogue's armchair analysis lacks.

## Primary sources

- Bürgerliches Gesetzbuch, Book 4 (Familienrecht) — FR1, FR5 in
  German statutory form.
- UN Convention on the Rights of the Child (1989) — the external
  constraint on FL1 (parental authority) that modern jurisdictions
  impose; the legal basis of the state's interface at FR6.

## Secondary sources

- [Minuchin1974] Minuchin, S. (1974). *Families and Family Therapy.*
  Harvard University Press. Structural family therapy: subsystems
  (spousal, parental, sibling), boundaries, and hierarchies — a
  near-isomorph of FL1–FL4, derived clinically.
- [Bowen1978] Bowen, M. (1978). *Family Therapy in Clinical
  Practice.* Jason Aronson. Family systems theory: emotional
  triangles and multigenerational transmission — feedback-loop
  constructs for the dynamics the SE decomposition renders
  statically.
- [Bronfenbrenner1979] Bronfenbrenner, U. (1979). *The Ecology of
  Human Development.* Harvard University Press. The nested
  micro/meso/exo/macro-system model — the standard developmental
  account of FR6/FF6 (the family's interfaces with school, state,
  community).
