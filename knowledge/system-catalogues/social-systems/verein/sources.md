# Verein (Voluntary Association) — Sources

Citations for the claims made in this folder. Keys not expanded here
resolve in `knowledge/references/bibliography.md`.

## Primary sources

- Bürgerliches Gesetzbuch §§ 21–79 (Vereinsrecht) — the entire
  VR-level in statutory form: legal personality via register entry
  (§ 21), the Vorstand (§ 26), the Mitgliederversammlung as supreme
  organ (§ 32), minority rights to convene it (§ 37).
- A model Satzung (any Landessportbund template) — VP1 as it is
  actually drafted.

## Secondary sources

- [Tocqueville1835] Tocqueville, A. de (1835/40). *De la démocratie
  en Amérique.* The classical argument for VG2/VG3: voluntary
  associations as the schools in which democratic skills are
  practised — why this system type matters beyond its own purposes.
- [Putnam1993] Putnam, R.D. (1993). *Making Democracy Work: Civic
  Traditions in Modern Italy.* Princeton University Press. The
  empirical case that associational density predicts the performance
  of surrounding democratic institutions.
- [Ostrom1990] — the design-principles mapping for the Verein's
  rung match is worked out in
  `../cross-system/justification-rungs-by-system.md`; Ostrom's
  principles are the field-derived counterpart of the BGB's designed
  structure.
- [Michels1911] — the standing caution: Michels' oligarchy data came
  from *voluntary organisations* (parties, unions). Small size and
  easy exit protect the Verein, but the drift he describes is the
  failure mode to watch when a Verein professionalises its Vorstand.

## Data sources

- ZiviZ-Survey (Zivilgesellschaft in Zahlen, Stifterverband) —
  periodic survey data on German associations; the empirical anchor
  for any claim about the type's prevalence or governance practice.
