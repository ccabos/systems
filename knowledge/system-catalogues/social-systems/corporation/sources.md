# Corporation (For-Profit) — Sources

Citations for the claims made in this folder. Keys not expanded here
resolve in `knowledge/references/bibliography.md`.

## Primary sources

- Aktiengesetz (AktG) and Mitbestimmungsgesetz (1976) — the German
  two-board implementation of CR2/CL1, including worker
  co-determination.
- Sarbanes–Oxley Act (2002) — the post-Enron restructuring of the
  audit loop (see `../cross-system/remedies-case-studies.md` §2).
- UK Corporate Governance Code (post-Cadbury 1992) — CR2 in
  comply-or-explain form.

## Secondary sources

- [Coase1937] Coase, R.H. (1937). "The Nature of the Firm."
  *Economica*, 4(16), 386–405. Why the corporation's boundary sits
  where it does — the transaction-cost answer to the boundary
  question `knowledge/foundations/boundaries-and-environment.md`
  asks structurally.
- [BerleMeans1932] Berle, A.A. & Means, G.C. (1932). *The Modern
  Corporation and Private Property.* Macmillan. The separation of
  ownership (CG1's principals) from control (CP2's managers) — the
  original identification of the accountability gap the SE analysis
  finds at CL1.
- [JensenMeckling1976] Jensen, M.C. & Meckling, W.H. (1976). "Theory
  of the Firm: Managerial Behavior, Agency Costs and Ownership
  Structure." *Journal of Financial Economics*, 3(4), 305–360.
  Agency theory: the economics formalisation of the
  controller-vs-interested-party problem that
  `knowledge/se-techniques/control-structures/dangerous-patterns.md`
  states in control terms.
- [Coates2007] and [Jager2021] — the evidence assessments for the
  SOX and Mitbestimmung remedy claims; see the tempering notes in
  `../cross-system/remedies-case-studies.md` §2.
