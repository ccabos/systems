# University — Sources

Citations for the claims made in this folder. Keys not expanded here
resolve in `knowledge/references/bibliography.md`.

## Primary sources

- Humboldt, W. von (1810). *Über die innere und äussere Organisation
  der höheren wissenschaftlichen Anstalten in Berlin.* The founding
  memorandum of the research university: unity of research and
  teaching (UG1+UG2), freedom of inquiry (UG4).
- Magna Charta Universitatum (1988) — the modern self-declaration of
  UG4 (institutional autonomy, academic freedom) signed by several
  hundred universities.

## Secondary sources

- [Merton1973] Merton, R.K. (1973). *The Sociology of Science:
  Theoretical and Empirical Investigations.* University of Chicago
  Press. The norms of science (communism, universalism,
  disinterestedness, organized scepticism) — the classical statement
  of the rung-3/4 standard UR1 claims, and the baseline against
  which the proxy-metric pathologies are deviations.
- [Clark1983] Clark, B.R. (1983). *The Higher Education System:
  Academic Organization in Cross-National Perspective.* University
  of California Press. The governance triangle (state authority,
  market, academic oligarchy) — the variation points of this system
  type across jurisdictions.
- [OSC2015], [Nosek2018], [DORA2013] — the reproducibility evidence
  and remedies cited in `../cross-system/remedies-case-studies.md`
  §4.
- [Strathern1997], [Muller2018] — the audit/metric pathology as it
  operates specifically on universities.
