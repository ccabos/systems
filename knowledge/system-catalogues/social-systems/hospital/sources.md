# Hospital — Sources

Citations for the claims made in this folder. Keys not expanded here
resolve in `knowledge/references/bibliography.md`.

## Primary sources

- Francis, R. (2013). *Report of the Mid Staffordshire NHS
  Foundation Trust Public Inquiry.* The Stationery Office (HC 898).
  [Francis2013] The evidentiary record behind the
  "manager dominance → proxy-metric substitution" observation: a
  hospital that hit its targets while care collapsed.
- Royal College of Physicians (2017). *National Early Warning Score
  (NEWS) 2.* RCP London. [RCP2017] The standardised physiological
  score — the implemented form of the "parallel rung-3 channel" for
  deterioration.

## Secondary sources

- [IOM2000] Institute of Medicine (2000). *To Err Is Human: Building
  a Safer Health System.* National Academies Press. The founding
  document of hospital patient safety as a *system* property rather
  than individual clinician failure — the healthcare field's own
  arrival at the STAMP position.
- [Hillman2005] Hillman, K. et al. (2005). "Introduction of the
  Medical Emergency Team (MET) System: A Cluster-Randomised
  Controlled Trial." *The Lancet*, 365(9477), 2091–2097. The MERIT
  trial — the largest RCT of rapid-response teams, and it was
  *null* on its primary outcomes. Essential caution for the F4
  observation: see the claim adaptation in `se-decomposition.md`.
- Vincent, C. (2010). *Patient Safety* (2nd ed.). Wiley-Blackwell.
  [Vincent2010] The standard textbook synthesis of the incident-
  reporting and safety-governance material behind R5/F7.

## Data sources

- Hogan, H. et al. (2012). "Preventable Deaths due to Problems in
  Care in English Acute Hospitals." *BMJ Quality & Safety*, 21(9),
  737–745. Case-record review estimating preventable in-hospital
  mortality — the magnitude anchor for "highest-stakes loop" claims.
