# Theocracy — Sources

Citations for the claims made in this folder. Keys not expanded here
resolve in `knowledge/references/bibliography.md`.

**Scope note.** The decomposition composites the Iranian
velayat-e-faqih state (the only large contemporary instance), the
historical Papal States, and smaller historical cases. The TP-level
examples mix these; no single system implements every element.

## Primary sources

- Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Iran (1979, am. 1989) —
  the one modern constitutional text that implements TR1 (sacred law
  supreme), TR2 (clerical qualification for governance, Art. 5,
  109), and TP1 (Supreme Leader, Council of Guardians) explicitly.
- *Codex Iuris Canonici* (1983) — canon law as a functioning
  religious-juridical subsystem (TL2) inside and formerly above a
  territorial state.

## Secondary sources

- [Weber1922] Weber's *hierocratic domination* is the ideal type
  this decomposition instantiates — his term for rule in which
  psychic coercion through the distribution or denial of religious
  benefits replaces or supplements physical coercion.
- [Arjomand1988] Arjomand, S.A. (1988). *The Turban for the Crown:
  The Islamic Revolution in Iran.* Oxford University Press. How the
  1979 revolution actually assembled the TL1–TL6 architecture; the
  empirical check on the composite.
